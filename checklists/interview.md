# Interview Checklist

Run through this before you end the conversation at step 0. If any box is empty, the interview
isn't done.

## Coverage
- [ ] Goal stated, and success has a number.
- [ ] At least one full scenario, start to finish.
- [ ] The unhappy path named.
- [ ] Data sources identified, with owners named.
- [ ] Functional behaviors listed, one at a time.
- [ ] Non-functional claims came back as numbers (p95, RPS, SLA, volume).
- [ ] Acceptance criteria the stakeholder would sign off on.

## Discipline
- [ ] Every "fast / reliable / accurate" was pushed to a number.
- [ ] Vague answers were left open, not resolved by you.
- [ ] The recording is running and audible.

## After
- [ ] Recording saved next to where the transcript will go.
- [ ] Anything unresolved noted for `open-questions.md`.
