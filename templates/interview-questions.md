# Interview Question Sheet

A live prompt sheet for step 0, so you don't drop an axis in the flow of conversation. Ask
in order, follow tangents, but come back and cover all six.

## 1. Goal and metric
- Why does this exist? What problem goes away if it works?
- How will you measure success? Give me a number.

## 2. Scenarios
- Walk me through a typical use, start to finish.
- Who starts it, what triggers it?
- What does the unhappy path look like?

## 3. Sources
- Where does the data come from? User, sensors, other systems?
- Who owns that data, how fresh is it?

## 4. Functional
- What must the system do? (Push for one behavior at a time.)
- What must it never do?

## 5. Non-functional
- How fast is acceptable? (Aim for p95.)
- How many users or requests at peak?
- How available does it need to be?
- How much data, growing how fast?

## 6. Acceptance
- How will you know it's correct?
- What would count as a failure in production?
- Who signs off?

Rule: every time you hear "fast," "reliable," or "accurate," ask for the number before moving on.
