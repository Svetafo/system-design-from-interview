## Flow

Rules: a step is one concrete action with real names (the endpoint called, the permission code in
the form `object:ACTION`, sub-numbering inside a step where needed). Error handling is phrased as
"If \<condition\>, then \<reaction or code\>". Audit events go in the Comment column, but only where
the requirements established an audit trail: inventing one here is exactly the drift this method
exists to prevent. What is unknown does not become TBD in a step, it goes to open questions.
